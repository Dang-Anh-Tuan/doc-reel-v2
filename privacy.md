# Privacy Policy — DocReel

_Last updated: 2026-11-24_

DocReel is a personal tool that turns text documents into short videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

## Data we access
- When the owner authorizes the app, it receives permission to upload videos and manage playlists on the owner's own YouTube channel (scopes `youtube.upload` and `youtube`).
- The app stores an OAuth refresh token, encrypted, on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google to obtain access tokens.

## Data we do not collect
- DocReel has no users other than its owner. It does not collect, sell, share or transmit personal data of any other person.
- No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking is used.

## Third-party services
Content of the owner's documents may be sent to a text-to-speech provider (ElevenLabs or Microsoft Edge TTS) to generate narration, and the finished video is uploaded to YouTube. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Use of Google user data
DocReel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local file `data/youtube-token.enc`.
